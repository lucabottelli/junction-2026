Speed & hardware

The last phase is about efficiency on Intel hardware: which model runs where, how long each step takes, and what it costs in power and heat. The idea is to spend compute only on the hard bricks.

Adaptive cascade

  1. Stage 1, fast: EfficientAd INT8 on the NPU. Clearly good or clearly defect exits here.
  2. Stage 2, accurate: PatchCore at a high-resolution crop on the iGPU, only for the unsure ones.
  3. Stage 3, active perception: still unsure, the arm brings the wrist camera close and scores again. Still unsure: reject the brick.

Calibration is the key risk: a stage-1 “good” exit must never miss a defect, so its threshold comes from the smallest crosses in the validation set.
