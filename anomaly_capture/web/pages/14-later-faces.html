Check every face

Bricks land on the orange plate at random and the cross can be on any of the six faces. The gripper always covers two of them, so a single photo is not enough. The inspection looks at the free faces first and stops at the first cross it finds.

How it will work

  1. Top camera checks the upward face before the pick. Free: that camera already locates the brick.
  2. More WP2 views: wrist roll and tilt show two sides and the bottom to the front camera.
  3. Only if still clean: set the brick down turned 90° at a fixed spot, pick it up again and show the two faces that were under the fingers.
  4. Stop early: a cross found at any step ends the inspection, so about 4 in 6 defective bricks skip the regrip.

What we tried

The tape-spot 90° re-grip exists as an experimental option under Record picks, but it is not reliable on the real arm yet (issue 12). Dropping the brick and letting it tumble was rejected: where it lands is random, so it would have to be found and picked again, and the views would never repeat.
