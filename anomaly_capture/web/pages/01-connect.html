Connect the arm and cameras

Three cameras watch the table: top looks down on the orange plate, front sees the brick in the gripper, wrist rides on the arm. You move the leader arm by hand; the follower copies it. This page gets all of them talking to the capture server.

What to do
  1. Close Studio's Environment view. The cameras and arms can only be used by one program at a time. Keep Studio's backend running, it is needed for training.
  2. Check that the three feeds below are live. Each caption says live, frozen or offline.
  3. Connect the follower arm, then put the leader roughly in the follower's pose.
  4. Choose Mirror leader and move the leader gently. The follower creeps until it has caught up, then follows.

Good to know

  • Mirror creeps at about 22°/s until the follower has caught up, so put the leader roughly in the follower's pose first.
  • Hold keeps the follower stiff where it is. Limp turns torque off: the arm drops.
  • Space or Esc, anywhere in this app, freezes the arm.
  • No camera feed or no arm after a replug? Run sudo setfacl -m u:intel:rw /dev/ttyACM*.
