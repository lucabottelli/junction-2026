What the models see

The cameras are fixed, but people walk past them. So the pick model never sees the room: it gets only the top camera, cropped to the table, and the wrist camera. The front camera is not a pick input at all; its anomaly photos keep only the gripper and brick, found by depth.

What to do
  1. Clear the table. Step back so no people, hands or arms are in the top camera's view.
  2. Drag a box tight around the orange plate and the area the gripper reaches. It is widened to 4:3 for you.
  3. Check the two pick views below: the whole plate is visible in the top view, and nothing outside the table is.
  4. Hold a brick at WP2 and check the masked front view: only the gripper and brick stay, the rest turns grey.
