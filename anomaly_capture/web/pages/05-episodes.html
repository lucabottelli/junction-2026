Step 5 of 7

Review episodes

Every pick is stored raw, exactly as recorded. ACT copies whatever it sees, so before training take a minute to switch off the picks you would not want it to imitate.

What to do
  1. Skim each session below: outcome, pick time and grasp attempts. Open a session to see its picks.
  2. Switch off Use on anything odd: a hesitant or wandering approach, a knocked block, a grip you would not repeat.
  3. Failed, missed and discarded picks are never used; there is nothing to do for them. Then continue to Train ACT.

Per block

Usable picks toward 30 with the block alone, 50 in total with distractors.
