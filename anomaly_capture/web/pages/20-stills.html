Manual stills

Still photos of a brick lying on the plate, from every camera at once, for the later top-camera check of the upward face (phase 5). Each capture averages several frames and saves the full frame plus a crop of the detected brick.

What to do
  1. Clear the plate of bricks and hands, then capture the empty background. Redo it whenever the light changes.
  2. Type the block name and put the brick on the plate.
  3. Capture good (no cross) or defect. Move and turn the brick between shots for variety.
  4. A bad shot? Undo last removes the most recent capture.
