    How many, how varied per block

    1. Round 1, block alone: 30–50 episodes all over the plate, every orientation (flat, turned, on its side, upside down, near the edge).
    2. Round 2, other blocks lying around as distractors: 20–30 episodes, same block name.
    3. Repeat for each of the 3 blocks.
    4. Check them under Episodes, then export and train.

    Consistent technique beats speed: the export removes hesitation and slowness anyway.

    When something goes wrong

    • Blocked, missed, timed out or discarded (X): the run pauses, it doesn't end. The robot copies the leader, slowly at first: move it clear, put the brick back on the orange plate, the leader back in its rest position, then press N.
    • Robot does something unexpected: press Space. Finished episodes are kept; Resume on the right continues the same session.
    • Stuck on Match: move each marker into the middle zone; press K if one joint can't get close.
    • Match takes long every time: re-teach the start pose with the leader lying in its rest position (Waypoints). Then the match is just setting the leader down.
    • Put-back lands badly: fine, it's just a new starting position.
