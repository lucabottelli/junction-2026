Inspect & sort

The robot shows the held object to the front camera. Any photo with a defect sends it straight to the defect plate. It goes to the good plate only after its whole surface was seen clean: first what is visible as picked, then it is set down on the tape spot, photographed from above and gripped again turned 90°, so what was under the fingers shows. Any shape works. Anything uncertain is never called good.

What to do
  1. Teach the two plates. Holding an object with the leader, save the release pose about 3 cm above each plate.
  2. Tick what each WP2 photo pose shows until the surface coverage reads 100 %. Front, back and down are the minimum, plus the tape spot.
  3. Choose how to pick and judge. Hand-feed with the manual judge tests every motion before a model exists; ACT policy picks by itself once a policy is trained and exported in Studio. Then press Start.
  4. Follow the cockpit. Hand-feed: put the object between the open fingers and press G. Manual judge: answer each photo with C, D or U.
  5. After training a model, calibrate the thresholds below, then switch the judge to Model.
