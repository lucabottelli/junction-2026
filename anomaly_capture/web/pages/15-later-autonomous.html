Run autonomously

The full loop without a human at the leader: pick a brick, carry it to WP2, decide, sort, next brick. It runs in our own runtime rather than Studio's, because it has to use exactly the same image crops as the recordings and hand over between the learned and the scripted parts.

The loop

  1. ACT pick with the same prep.py crops as during recording.
  2. Grip detected by the same stall-based hand-off as in collection, then switch to the scripted carry to WP2.
  3. Anomaly inference at WP2, then sort onto the good or defect plate.
  4. Next brick. STOP on this page (Space / Esc) freezes the arm at any moment.
