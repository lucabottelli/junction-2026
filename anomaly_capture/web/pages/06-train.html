Step 6 of 8

Clean the demos & train ACT

A human teleoperator hesitates and fumbles, and ACT copies whatever it sees. The export keeps the best version of every pick, then writes a Studio dataset with the top and wrist cameras.

What to do
  1. Preview first: the log lists which picks are kept, which are rejected and why.
  2. Export to Studio once the preview looks right. Raw episodes are never changed; re-export any time.
  3. Train in Studio: ACT on the Intel GPU, cameras top + wrist (below).

What the cleaning does

Four passes over every raw pick, in this order.

  1. 01Reject

    Failed, missed, discarded or switched-off picks; fumbles with more grasp attempts than allowed; picks slower than 2.5× the median; camera stalls over 0.5 s.

  2. 02Trim

    The idle time before you start moving the leader.

  3. 03Smooth

    Hand tremor out of the joint targets (Gaussian, σ 1.5 frames). The gripper is left untouched.

  4. 04Retime

    Pauses shrink 10×, free-space motion is sped up, and no joint moves more than 6° per step.
