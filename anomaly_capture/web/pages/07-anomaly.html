Step 7 of 8

Anomaly data

Front-camera photos of each block held in the gripper at waypoint 2. They are taken automatically during recording and later train one good / defect model per block.

Capture all good data before drawing any cross. The pen is permanent: once a block has a cross it can never give good images again.
What to do
  1. Hand-feed below: pick the block and Good, press Get ready, put the block in the open clamp, press Close clamp. The arm photographs it at every WP2 pose and comes back open.
  2. Turn the block between handlings (another face to the camera, other grip height) and repeat until each block has at least 60 good images.
  3. Only then draw crosses of different sizes and capture 10–20 images per block with label Defect.
