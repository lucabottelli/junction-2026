Step 7 of 8

Anomaly data

Front-camera photos of each block held in the gripper at waypoint 2. They are taken automatically during recording and later train one good / defect model per block.

Capture all good data before drawing any cross. The pen is permanent: once a block has a cross it can never give good images again.
What to do
  1. Switch on the WP2 photos under Record picks → Options.
  2. Record every clean block with label Good until it has at least 60 in-hand images.
  3. Only then draw crosses of different sizes and record 10–20 images per block with label Defect.
