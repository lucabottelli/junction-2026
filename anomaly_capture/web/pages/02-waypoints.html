Step 2 of 8

Teach the waypoints

The robot replays a few fixed poses around the pick it learns. You show it each pose once with the leader arm and save it. Two are required; the rest are optional.

What to do
  1. Turn on Mirror leader. The follower copies the leader arm live.
  2. Move the leader into the pose described on the card below. For a pose that holds a brick, close the leader's gripper on it.
  3. Save current pose on that card. To fix it later, move again and press Set.
  4. Check it with Go: the follower drives there slowly. Go switches the arm to Hold, so turn Mirror back on before teaching the next pose.
